About Me
======
I am a MS/Ph.D student in the Department of Semiconductor System Engineering at Korea University. I conduct research in GPU architecture at the Computer Architecture and System Software Lab (ComSys), advised by Professor Yunho Oh.

My research interest lies in GPU architecture and high performance GPU computing.
